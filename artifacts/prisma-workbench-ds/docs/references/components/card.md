# Card family

## Source evidence

- `artifacts/litmatrix/src/App.tsx`: page header, navigation summary, and
  stage content surfaces.
- Every component under `artifacts/litmatrix/src/components/`: rounded white
  panels with slate borders and restrained shadows.

## Extracted behavior and visual rules

Cards group one review concern at a time. Use a white surface over the
slate-50 page background, a thin slate border, and rounded-lg or rounded-xl
corners. Keep headings distinct from metadata and use generous inner padding
for evidence text and tables.
